# Phase 8 contract

Phase 8 may extend Phase 7 only through these stable interfaces. Do not replace the temporal
kernel, FEC, research-object store or economic claim ontology with a second platform.

## Stable interfaces

* `atlas.economic.intelligence.v1` — catalog and source/rights contract.
* `atlas.economic.panel.v1` — aligned observations, as-of mode, completeness, quality.
* `atlas.economic.graph.v1` — entities, edges, claims, conflicts, provenance and rights.
* `atlas.economic.claim.v1` — epistemic/causal status and source evidence.
* `atlas.economic.weather_demand.v1` — split, candidates, validation/test, audit and causal
  boundary.
* `atlas.economic.scenario.v1` — shock, demand, financial, uncertainty, sensitivity and
  fingerprint.
* `atlas.economic.replay.v1` — exact reproduction or explicit `insufficient_evidence`.
* `atlas.economic.sentinel.v1` — freshness, dependencies, stale runs, alerts and manual
  automatic-action flags.
* `atlas.research_object.v1` — dataset, model, experiment and application versions.
* `atlas.model.v1` and `atlas.fec.compiler.v1` — authoritative financial execution.
* `atlas.forecast.v1` — forecast object with data class and null decision rule.

## Rules for Phase 8

1. **No silent promotion.** A hypothesis, association or LLM extraction becomes observed only
   through explicit evidence and—where relevant—a reviewed promotion record.
2. **No invented vintage.** Historical replay must use evidenced publication/forecast archives
   or refuse. Current bulk downloads cannot be relabeled as as-issued history.
3. **No invented economic relationship.** Company, facility, supplier and contract edges need
   source evidence or an explicit hypothetical flag.
4. **No second financial engine.** Scenario-to-finance work must compile through the FEC.
5. **Uncertainty stays decomposed.** Do not collapse observation, parameter, model-form and
   identification uncertainty into one unexplained number.
6. **Causal labels require design.** A regression with controls does not qualify. DiD/ITS/
   synthetic control require stated assumptions and falsification tests.
7. **Source rights persist.** Exports and derived artifacts retain provider, licence and
   redistribution constraints.
8. **Dependencies are explicit.** A revision invalidates affected runs; it never silently
   rewrites an application.
9. **Monitoring is honest.** “Live” requires an implemented scheduler and delivery runtime.
10. **No live trading or financial execution** without a separately authorized phase.
11. **Untrusted text stays untrusted.** It cannot grant itself tools, filesystem access,
   permissions or model configuration.
12. **Measured claims only.** Benchmarks record hardware, workload and cold/warm semantics.

## Required source of truth

The genuine `ATLAS_MASTER_SPEC.md` v1.2.0 must be supplied before Phase 8 can claim conformance
to its detailed event, OSINT, domain-pack, real-estate and institutional-benchmark sections.
The current in-repo contract set is an interim authority and must be reconciled, not silently
overwritten.

## Definition of done for Phase 8

A capability requires an executable path, versioned storage, a provenance-bearing result, an
independent numerical or behavioral test, a rights/timing statement, a measured benchmark where
performance is claimed, and a limitation when the evidence is insufficient. A chart, a static
mock, or a generated narrative is not a capability.

## Non-goals

Live orders, custody, unrestricted web scraping, private-location ingestion, autonomous capital
allocation, distributed compute without measured need, and causal language without identification.

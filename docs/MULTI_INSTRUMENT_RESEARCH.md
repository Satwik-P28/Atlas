# Multi-instrument research

## What exists

`atlas.institutional.universe` publishes a deterministic four-instrument fixture with
stable IDs, tickers, sectors, currencies, membership dates and listing status:

| ID | Sector | Membership | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SYN-ALPHA` | Technology | 2021-01-04 → present | active |
| `SYN-BETA` | Industrials | 2021-01-04 → present | active |
| `SYN-GAMMA` | Defensives | 2021-06-01 → present | active |
| `SYN-DELTA` | Energy | 2021-01-04 → 2023-12-29 | delisted |

Prices are correlated geometric paths generated by a documented deterministic LCG and
Box-Muller transform. The calendar is weekdays only; exchange holidays are not modeled.
The panel is not a market data product.

The active return matrix is 935 daily observations from 2021-01-05 through 2024-12-31.
A day enters the matrix only when every active security has a current and previous price.
`SYN-DELTA` remains visible in the universe and membership history, but is not silently
forward-filled or included in an active portfolio.

## Why synthetic

The local database contains real IBM vendor bars, but no legally usable multi-security
price history. Fabricating a “real” cross-section would violate the project's data rules.
The fixture isolates engineering and mathematical behavior: alignment, delisting, covariance,
optimization and walk-forward selection. Every response carries:

```
data_class: synthetic_fixture
universe_id: SYN-UNIVERSE-PHASE5
content_hash: dee661837022b965b981d99212da7398af60a00f729c2fe9e7552dbbdc780dfe
```

The hash is over the complete generated panel, so a fixture change invalidates saved
research comparisons.

## Instrument identity and valuation

Instrument identity (`security_id`, membership, ticker, sector, currency) is separate
from valuation. Only cash equities with deterministic prices are valued by this engine.
Bonds in the fixed-income lab are hypothetical instruments valued from a dated curve;
they are not registered in the equity backtester. Options are analytical model values and
are not portfolio holdings.

This preserves the rule: registered identity is not a claim of executable accounting.

## Portfolio semantics

The research engine rebalances target weights at a close and applies the next session's
cross-sectional simple return. A turnover cost is charged on the next day. It records
weights, turnover and dates. It is **not** the Phase 4 event-driven ledger and does not
claim next-open fills, share quantities, dividends or cash accounting.

Risk uses the final `lookback` aligned observations (default 252). Covariance is sample
covariance and annualized by 252. A separate report with all 935 observations is available
for comparison, but no lookback is mixed inside one calculation.

## Adding a real universe

A real provider would need: versioned membership with effective dates, delisting returns,
corporate actions, exchange calendars, currency and a redistribution licence. None of
those are present. The API and schemas are extensible, but no production claim should be
made until a provider is selected and audited.

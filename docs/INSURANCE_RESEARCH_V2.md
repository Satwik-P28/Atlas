# Insurance research v2

UnitedHealth is a consolidated insurer and healthcare-services business. The fixture has no
mapped premium, medical-cost, claims-reserve, membership, segment or CMS plan-level fields.
The model therefore remains a justified-earnings valuation with a residual cross-check and is
labelled **partial**.

No medical loss ratio, reserve adequacy or Optum sum-of-the-parts value is fabricated. The
`health_insurance` block reports `not_available_in_fixture` with the missing evidence named.
A later phase must join CMS and segment disclosures before claiming a complete insurer model.

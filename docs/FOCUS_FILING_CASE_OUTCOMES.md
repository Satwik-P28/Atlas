## Period-assignment audit (Coca-Cola FY2022)

FOCUS-01 was previously recorded with `column_label = '2021'` beside `period = '2022'`. That is a
real semantic defect, not a labelling slip, and it is now reported rather than certified.

**What the stored table actually contains.** The flattened header row holds four cells
(`Year Ended December 31, | 2022 | 2021 | 2020`) while the data row holds ten
(`Net Operating Revenues | $ | 43,004 | '' | $ | 38,655 | '' | $ | 33,014 | ''`), because the source
uses `colspan` groups and the flattening keeps empty cells inconsistently. Indexing the short header
row at the data column therefore lands on `2021` while the value is 2022's.

**Independent HTML geometry.** Re-parsing the same bytes with `colspan` expansion shows three
three-column year groups (currency marker, amount, blank), which places `43,004` in the `2022` group.
The flattening, not the filing, is what misaligned the label.

**Independent XBRL evidence.** SEC company facts for CIK 0000021344, accession
`0000021344-23-000011`: FY2022 revenues `43,004,000,000`, FY2021 `38,655,000,000`, FY2020
`33,014,000,000`. The printed `43,004` is the FY2022 figure, so `period = '2022'` is economically
correct.

**What the certificate now says.** The verifier reports `period_assignment: unresolved` for FOCUS-01
(also FOCUS-03, FOCUS-04 and FOCUS-05, which bind the same columns), with a limitation naming the
conflicting header. The cell, its coordinates and the arithmetic remain `SOURCE_VERIFIED`; the meaning
of the period is not certified by Atlas. FOCUS-02's aligned header is `header_confirmed`; JPMorgan and
3M tables print no period in the selected column header, so their status is `not_determined`.

Repairing the extractor's header alignment would let these cases certify the period from geometry.
That is a parser change with a wider blast radius than this correction, and it was deliberately not
made here.

## Refused cases

### FOCUS-09 — JPMorgan gross profit (justified unanswerability)

The income statement as filed does not present a bindable `gross profit` line for JPMorgan. The resolver
reports no candidate rather than substituting a nearby subtotal. This refusal is the policy working: a
bank's income statement does not have the retail-style gross profit structure the question assumes.

### FOCUS-12 — 3M FY2018 net sales (resolver capability gap)

The FY2018 10-K prints consolidated net sales of `32,765`. Atlas does not resolve it. The refusal record
carries the observed printed value precisely so that this case cannot later be read as "abstained, and
therefore fine". It is a defect in table/concept resolution against an older statement layout.

Not counted as an answered question.

### FOCUS-13 — 3M net sales at a 2017 cutoff (historical information refusal)

The question is evaluated at a cutoff before the FY2022 filing was public. `lookup_bound` refuses with a
future-evidence problem. Correct temporal behaviour: no materialised answer from the future is allowed to
leak backwards.

## What would move FOCUS-12 to answered

A resolver that recognises the FY2018 statement layout and its row label, plus a scale banner read from
that filing's header row, then a manifest cell for `(table_sha256, row, column)` covering the new cell.
Until then the case stays a refusal, and the report keeps saying so.

**Evidence limitation:** Several Coca-Cola FY2022 cells have a flattened-table `column_label` of `2021` despite a normalized `period` of `2022`. The printed values and arithmetic were checked, but the certificate does not independently establish the fiscal period encoded by merged HTML headers. Original-statement inspection and XBRL/geometry corroboration remain necessary.
